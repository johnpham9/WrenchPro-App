WrenchPro Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 7, 2026

WrenchPro is an offline-first field service application for independent HVAC technicians. This policy explains what information the app handles, where that information is stored, and when it may leave the app.

## Information You Enter

WrenchPro may store information that you choose to enter, including:

- Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, service addresses, and notes
- Equipment brands, models, serial numbers, installation details, warranty information, and service history
- Service visit dates, complaints, diagnoses, work performed, parts, HVAC readings, refrigerant activity, and technician notes
- Invoice or estimate details, prices, taxes, discounts, and paid or unpaid status
- Truck stock item names, quantities, costs, locations, and notes
- Before-and-after service photos selected from your photo library or captured with the camera
- Customer signatures captured within the app
- Your selected app language

WrenchPro does not require a WrenchPro account and does not collect passwords, payment card information, government identifiers, or precise location data.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the app's features, including maintaining customer and equipment history, documenting service visits, comparing readings, flagging potential callbacks, managing truck stock, and generating service reports and invoices.

WrenchPro does not use your information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-app tracking.

## Storage And iCloud Sync

WrenchPro keeps a local copy of app data on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

On supported physical devices, WrenchPro can synchronize app records through Apple's CloudKit service using the private database associated with the Apple Account signed into iCloud on the device. This may include customer records, equipment data, service visits, readings, refrigerant logs, invoice details, truck stock, photos, and signatures.

The developer does not operate a separate WrenchPro server or user-account database. iCloud storage and synchronization are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy. Simulator builds do not use CloudKit.

## Photos And Camera

When you choose a photo, Apple's system photo picker lets you select which images WrenchPro can import. When you use the camera, the captured image is added to the service visit. Imported and captured images are stored as part of WrenchPro's app data and may synchronize through your private iCloud database.

WrenchPro does not scan your entire photo library or upload photos to a developer-operated server.

## Signatures

Customer signatures are stored as image data associated with a service visit. They may appear in generated service reports and may synchronize through the user's private iCloud database. Obtain the customer's permission before capturing or sharing a signature.

## Reports And Sharing

WrenchPro can create PDF service reports. A report may contain customer information, equipment details, service notes, readings, invoice information, photos, and a signature.

Reports are shared only when you initiate sharing and choose a destination through Apple's native share sheet. Once shared, the selected recipient, application, or service controls the copy it receives under its own privacy practices. Temporary report files may be created on the device while preparing a share operation and are subject to iOS temporary-file management.

## Analytics, Advertising, And Tracking

The current version of WrenchPro does not include:

- Third-party analytics SDKs
- Advertising SDKs
- Cross-app tracking
- Data brokers
- A developer-operated backend
- Automatic sale or sharing of personal information

Apple may process diagnostic, App Store, purchase, iCloud, or device information under the settings of your device and Apple Account. That processing is governed by Apple's policies and is not controlled by WrenchPro.

## Data Retention And Deletion

App data remains on the device and, when enabled, in the user's private iCloud database until it is deleted by the user or removed under Apple's storage policies.

Deleting individual customers, equipment, visits, photos, signatures, or inventory records in WrenchPro removes those records from the app and synchronizes the deletion when iCloud is available. Deleting the app removes its local data, but synchronized data may remain in iCloud and may return after reinstallation. Users can manage iCloud app data through Apple device settings.

Before deleting records, consider exporting any reports that must be retained for business, warranty, tax, or regulatory purposes.

## Data Security

WrenchPro relies on iOS data protection and Apple's iCloud and CloudKit security controls. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect the device and Apple Account with a strong passcode, two-factor authentication, and current system software.

## Your Responsibilities

WrenchPro is a tool for storing business records. You are responsible for:

- Having an appropriate reason and permission to store customer information
- Protecting access to your device and Apple Account
- Obtaining permission before capturing or sharing photos and signatures
- Meeting applicable recordkeeping, refrigerant, tax, consumer, and privacy requirements
- Reviewing reports before sharing them with customers or third parties

## Children's Privacy

WrenchPro is a professional field service tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

## Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when WrenchPro's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this document will be revised when an updated policy is published.

## Contact

Privacy questions or requests should be sent through the WrenchPro support contact listed on its App Store product page.
